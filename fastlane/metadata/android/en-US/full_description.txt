Pixora is an ad-free, open-source camera app built to get more out of your phone's camera.

🌙 NIGHT MODE
In the dark, Pixora captures a burst of frames instead of one, merges them while protecting moving areas, and removes noise intelligently. The result: brighter, cleaner night photos.

✨ ASTRO MODE
Put your phone on a tripod or a steady surface and Pixora takes several long exposures of the night sky, removes hot pixels and merges them into a clean star photo. Shown only on phones that support long exposures.

👤 PORTRAIT MODE
An on-device AI depth map separates your subject from the background and applies a natural background blur.

🎛️ PRO MODE
Exposure compensation, white balance, color profiles (Natural, Monochrome, Flat Log) and a live histogram. On phones that allow it, ISO, shutter speed and manual focus are unlocked too.

📸 AND MORE
• Panorama: gyroscope-guided capture with automatic stitching
• Video: up to 4K where your phone supports it, H.264 or H.265
• Time-lapse
• Switch between ultra-wide, main and 2x with one tap
• Tap to focus and expose, long-press for AF/AE lock
• Grid (3x3, square, golden ratio), timer, flash
• Shoot with the volume keys
• RAW (DNG) capture on supported phones
• Scene detection

🔒 PRIVACY
Pixora has no internet permission. Your photos and videos never leave your phone. All AI processing happens on the device. Location tagging is optional and off by default.

⚙️ COMPATIBILITY
Android 9 and newer. Some features (RAW, manual ISO/shutter, 60 FPS video, video stabilization) depend on whether your phone's camera hardware exposes them to apps; Pixora automatically hides options your phone doesn't support.

Note: the app interface is currently in Turkish. English is coming soon.

Pixora is open source: github.com/Marque-source/Pixora
